Mobile checkout converts at 1.1%, against 2.9% on desktop — and mobile is 60% of your traffic. You suspect the payment step but aren't certain. The June theme relaunch didn't move the number, and the upsell app you tried has already been uninstalled. You need mobile "at least close to desktop" before the Black Friday creative goes live on 7 November, without a rebrand and without touching the payment provider (locked in until March).

Request 1 — Desktop checkout "to match" · Out of scope

Hi Priya,

Glad it's landing well.

Both good ideas — worth flagging that they sit outside what we scoped in the 21 September SOW, so here's how I'd handle them:

Desktop checkout matching: the SOW scopes this engagement to mobile specifically, since desktop's already at 2.9%. If you'd like it done anyway for consistency: 1.5 days · €975.
Apple Pay: this wasn't one of the audit's priority fixes, and touches payment provider setup, which the SOW also carves out. Adding it: 1.5 days · €975.
